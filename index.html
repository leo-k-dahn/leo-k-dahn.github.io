My story

Curiosity gave me a place to start.
Focus gives me a way forward.

I am a young Liberian from Monrovia. I have always been curious, playful, and interested in understanding how things work — the kind of person who takes something apart just to see how it goes back together.

My journey has included challenges, delays, learning experiences, leadership opportunities, and moments that tested my determination. None of it followed a straight line, and I've had to make peace with that. Falling behind in school could have discouraged me — instead it taught me patience, and it made the progress I've made since feel earned rather than assumed.

My story is not about having everything figured out. It is about continuing to learn, improving myself, and moving forward despite setbacks — one practical skill, one lesson, and one project at a time.
